# Reflections Log

Short, frequent entries. Honest and quick beats polished and rare. Add entries at the top, most recent first.

## Entry types

- [EVENT] something happened worth capturing
- [THINKING] a mental model, hypothesis or shift in perspective
- [BOOK] an excerpt or concept, with a note on why it matters to you
- [SIGNAL] something small you noticed that may matter later
- [FRICTION] something bothering you, even if you cannot resolve it yet

## Format

### YYYY-MM-DD, [TYPE], Title

2 to 6 sentences. What happened or what is the idea? Why does it matter? What question does it leave open?

Tags (optional): #retention #client-health #delegation #early-signal
