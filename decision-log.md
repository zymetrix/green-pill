# Decision Log

Record significant decisions at the time of deciding. Update with the outcome later. The value is in the honesty of the reasoning, not the quality of the outcome.

Qualifies: anything that affects people, clients, strategy or finances in a non-trivial way, or any decision you lost sleep over.

Add new entries at the top, most recent first.

---

## Entry format

### [DL-001] Short decision title

**Date of decision:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Domain:** People / Strategy / Client / Finance / Operations / Product
**Reversibility:** Easy / Hard / Irreversible
**Stakes:** Low / Medium / High

**Situation**
2 to 4 sentences. Context, and what made this a decision rather than routine.

**Options considered**
- Option A:
- Option B:
- Option C:

**Decision made**
One or two sentences.

**Reasoning at the time**
The most important field. Your actual logic, data, gut sense or principle. What you were most uncertain about. What you were assuming.

**Who was involved**
Who gave input, who decided, who was affected but not consulted, and why.

**What I was feeling**
Optional. Pressure, doubt, confidence, urgency.

**Predicted outcome**
Specific enough to evaluate later.

## Example (fictional, delete before use)

### [DL-000] Promote a top engineer to team lead without a trial period

**Date of decision:** 2025-03-10
**Domain:** People
**Reversibility:** Hard
**Stakes:** High

**Situation**
A strong engineer had been informally leading a small team for six months. A lead role opened up and several people expected him to get it.

**Options considered**
- A: Promote now.
- B: 90-day structured trial with explicit criteria.
- C: Hire externally and offer a specialist track.

**Decision made**
Promote now (A).

**Reasoning at the time**
He had earned it and the team respected him. A trial felt bureaucratic and demotivating. I trusted my read of him. Main uncertainty: handling difficult conversations. I assumed he would figure it out.

**Predicted outcome**
Smooth transition and better retention.

**Outcome update:** Mixed. Technical performance held, but an underperformer went unaddressed for four months. I had treated technical leadership as proof of people leadership. Next time: a 60-day trial with one explicit people-management scenario and coaching from day one.

**Pattern tags:** #trusted-relationship-over-data #assumption-not-tested
